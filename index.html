About Us

Hello and welcome to One Love Radio - the radio station of choice where people with like-minded ideas meet and mingle, where passion meets purpose. We bring you the best in music, entertainment, and education.

Founded in November 2024, One Love Radio was created to bring together joy, love, and happiness to its fans. Whether you are tuning in for the music, the stories, or the vibes, you are part of a community that celebrates and connects everyone. Our live broadcasting starts from 6pm SA Time daily.

Give us a try and let us know what you think. One Love Radio - a station of pure love and entertainment.
